LIGHT, NOT HYPE.

Visible light can influence vision and circadian signaling. Effects depend on spectrum, intensity, timing, duration, viewing distance and the display itself.

Blue/cyan light at night can suppress melatonin and increase alerting responses. Warmer amber/red light generally produces less melanopic stimulation; it does not “add melatonin.”

This phone cannot generate UVB or therapeutic near-infrared, and no preset claims those effects.
